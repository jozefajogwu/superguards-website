About Superguards

Protection you can see. Discipline you can trust.

Superguards Ltd is a private security company based in Jos, Plateau State. Our clients include commercial banks, businesses and private individuals.

We have over 150 trained security officers in the field, supported by about 20 administrative and accounting staff. Our executive directors include retired military and police officers with extensive experience in security matters.

We recruit only experienced, capable staff, and every new recruit goes through training in the Superguards operational system before deployment. Every officer we deploy is background-checked, trained and supervised by field managers who visit your site regularly. Our control room tracks every post around the clock, so problems are spotted early and handled fast.

  • Thorough background screening
  • Continuous officer training
  • Site-specific security plans
  • Daily incident reporting
  • Led by ex-military & police officers
  • Transparent monthly billing

FAQs

Common questions

Are your guards licensed and trained?

Yes. Superguards has been licensed by the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) since March 2007, licence No. NSCDC/PG/007/00029 (Category B), and every officer is screened and trained before deployment, with refresher training throughout their service.

How quickly can you deploy officers?

After the site assessment and agreement, we can usually deploy within a few days. For urgent cases, talk to us and we'll do our best to cover you sooner.

Do you cover short-term or one-off events?

Yes. We provide event security for single days or weekends, as well as long-term monthly contracts for homes and businesses.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on the number of officers, shift hours, site risk level and any equipment needed. After the free assessment you get a clear, itemised quote with no hidden charges.

Which locations do you serve?

We provide nationwide coverage, deploying officers to sites anywhere in Nigeria. Our office is at No. 8B Rukuba Road, Jos, Plateau State, and our team will plan deployment and supervision for your location.
